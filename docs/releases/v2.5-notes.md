# v2.5 release notes (draft)

Lines to pull into the v2.5 release. Other v2.5 work adds its own.

## Privacy and settings

- **Keep local travels with your meetings.** Each meeting's folder now records whether it's kept local (and whether its folders are). If the app ever rebuilds its library from the meeting folders, or you copy a meeting folder to another computer, Keep local comes back with it.
- **Recovered meetings you should review.** When the app restores a meeting whose folder doesn't record a Keep local setting (older folders), it sets the meeting to Keep local to be safe and shows a notice so you can review it and turn Keep local off where it isn't needed.
- **A new AI server needs your OK again.** Changing the server for OpenAI or NVIDIA (Settings › Ask and Enhance › More options › Base URL) now asks for the API key again and asks again before anything is sent to the new server. The prompt names the server.

## Local AI

- **The local AI model fits your computer.** A new install picks its local model from the computer's memory: `qwen3.5:9b` from 16 GB, `llama3.1` at 12 GB, `qwen3.5:4b` below that. Settings › Ask and Enhance shows each model's size and what was measured for it (answers backed by a source, declining what it can't answer, seconds per answer), on one test laptop with other work running. You confirm the download. `qwen3.5:9b` is the better model but slower where it isn't live: the notes, cleanup and action items made after a meeting take about 1.5 to 2.5 times as long as with `llama3.1`, and Settings says so beside it.
- **A better model is offered, never forced.** A computer already using `llama3.1` that can run `qwen3.5:9b` sees a one-time offer in Settings › Ask and Enhance. Nothing downloads or switches until you click "Download and switch", and "Not now" ends the offer. A model you picked yourself is never changed or offered over, and nothing is offered for an Ollama running on another computer.
- **Downloads check for room and for Ollama's version first.** If the drive hasn't got the download plus 2 GB to spare, Settings says how much is needed and how much is free instead of starting. A download that was interrupted only needs the part still to come. A model that needs a newer Ollama than the one you run (`qwen3.5` needs 0.17.5) is held back with "update Ollama to use it", not offered and not downloaded.

## Storage

- **A backup of your library is saved before each update.** Before an update changes your notes and transcripts, Fly on the Wall saves a copy of its database in the `backups` folder inside your data folder, and keeps the newest 3 (recordings and attachments aren't copied; a note you delete later stays in older copies until they're replaced; Settings › Your files and privacy › Backups opens the folder). To go back to one: quit the app, delete `flyonthewall.db-wal` and `flyonthewall.db-shm` in the data folder, copy the backup over `flyonthewall.db`, and start the app. If there isn't enough free disk space, the update goes ahead and you're told once that no backup was saved. "Delete all my data" removes the backups too.
- **Recordings take much less space, losslessly.** Finished recordings are stored as FLAC at their original quality, checked sample for sample before the original WAV goes; playback and transcripts are exactly as before. It runs in the background and steps aside while you record, transcribe or use Ask.
- **Opt-in: imported videos can keep only their audio, and screen recordings can shrink.** Settings › Your files and privacy. Keeping audio only removes the picture (and any subtitles) of the app's copy; the audio is copied out exactly as it is. Shrinking re-encodes a screen recording only when the result looks the same and is clearly smaller. Both ask first.

## Upgrade notes

- **Managed Ollama: new installs get v0.40.13 on Windows; existing installs keep the runtime they have.** The managed runtime's folder isn't versioned, so an install made earlier stays on its current Ollama (v0.31.2) until it is reinstalled. macOS stays on v0.31.2 for now.
- **Don't go back to v2.4 after upgrading.** v2.4 can rewrite a meeting folder's record in its older format, which drops the Keep local information v2.5 stores there. Nothing is deleted, and v2.5 treats such a folder as kept local if it ever has to restore it, but you may then need to turn Keep local off again for those meetings.
- **After going back to v2.4, audio retention misses `.mka` files.** If imported videos were set to keep only their audio, some of that audio may be stored as `.mka` (Matroska audio), which v2.4's audio retention doesn't recognize, so it won't delete those files when their meeting's audio expires. Delete them by hand from the meeting's folder, or stay on v2.5.
